Rally Startup Festival 2026

Welcome to the Startup Innovation City — the grand final festival of the Rally Egypt Entrepreneurship competition. Step into six immersive districts designed to empower the next generation of founders, creators, and innovators.

Wednesday, September 30, 2026
AlBurouj Cultural Hub, New Heliopolis
Free Admission (Registration Required)
Register for Free @rallystartupfestival
Innovation City

The 6 Interactive Districts

As showcased on Instagram (@rallystartupfestival), the venue transforms into a complete startup city covering every stage of the entrepreneurial journey.

District 01

Innovation Village

The epicenter of research and breakthrough tech. Discover cutting-edge graduation projects, working engineering prototypes, AI-driven solutions, and deep-tech inventions from universities across Egypt.

  • University Research Inventions
  • Robotics, AI & Hardware Demos
  • Commercial Feasibility Showcases
District 02

Founder Factory

An intensive workshop machine for founders and executives. Dive into masterclasses covering fundraising mindset, business model validation, team scaling, and investor pitch preparation.

  • Fund Mindset & Capital Raising
  • Leadership & Founder Etiquette
  • Energy & Productivity Optimization
District 03

Go Viral

Unlocking the power of modern storytelling, content marketing, and organic growth. Learn how to craft viral campaigns, build founder personal branding, and create studio-grade mobile video content.

  • Mobile Filmmaking & Content Creation
  • Growth Hacking & Social Reach
  • Brand Positioning & PR Strategy
District 04

The Marketplace

The buzzing commercial bazaar of the festival. Student startups set up dynamic exhibition booths to interact directly with early adopters, demo their apps, sell tangible goods, and capture customer feedback.

  • Live Product Sales & Demos
  • Direct User Testing & Acquisition
  • B2B & B2C Commercial Exchange
District 05

Rally Square

The heart and main arena of the festival city. Featuring keynote addresses by Egyptian corporate titans, fiery panel debates on the startup ecosystem, and the championship final pitch battles.

  • Headlining Corporate Keynotes
  • Grand Pitch Finals & Defense
  • National Awards Ceremony & Trophy Gala
District 06

Festival District

Where connection meets celebration. Unwind in relaxing networking pods, enjoy student society activations, take commemorative photos at interactive installations, and build lifelong friendships.

  • Ecosystem Networking Lounges
  • Live Entertainment & Fun Zones
  • Student Chapter Activations
